# The owner's second test sheet

The first sheet stopped being a blind test once the ways of writing a 4, a 1 and a 7 were taught after looking at it.
This one is written after those choices and only read, never studied for new training data: it measures the models as
they are.

## How to write it

1. On the tablet: My books > New > New canvas note, named `Erkennungstest 2`, on the paper you usually use.
2. Write the formulas below with the pen, **in this order, one per line**, the way you normally write. Leave out the
   numbers, and leave about one empty line between formulas.
3. Correct mistakes as usual (erase or scribble out). Only the finished ink counts.
4. Open the Windows app and let it sync. Notes sync among devices, and the sheet is read from there.
